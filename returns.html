🔄 Returns & Refunds

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If something isn't right, we're here to help!

📅

30-Day Return Window

You have 30 days from delivery date to return most items. Electronics have a 14-day return window.

💰

Full Refund

Refunds are processed to your original payment method (Telebirr, Bank Transfer, or Card) within 5-7 business days.

📦

Free Return Shipping

Free return shipping for defective or incorrect items. Return shipping fees apply for "changed mind" returns.

How to Return an Item

1

Initiate Return

Fill out the return form below with your order details and reason for return.

2

Pack Your Item

Package the item securely in its original packaging with all accessories and tags.

3

Ship & Receive Refund

Ship the item back to us. Once received, we'll process your refund within 5-7 business days.

❌ Non-Returnable Items

  • Perishable goods (food, spices, teff, coffee beans)
  • Personalized or custom-made items
  • Digital products and downloadable content
  • Items damaged due to misuse
