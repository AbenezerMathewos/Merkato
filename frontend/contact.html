💡 Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?

You can track your order in the My Orders section. You'll also receive tracking information via SMS and email once your order ships.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Telebirr mobile money, Cash on Delivery (COD), Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard), and Bank Transfer.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery within Addis Ababa takes 1-3 business days. Regional deliveries take 3-7 business days.
