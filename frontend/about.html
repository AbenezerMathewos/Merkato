🌟 About MERKATO

We are Ethiopia's premier digital marketplace, connecting authentic local products with modern convenience. Our mission is to celebrate Ethiopian heritage while embracing the future of commerce.

🌱

Authenticity

Every product is carefully sourced from Ethiopian artisans, farmers, and manufacturers. We guarantee 100% authentic Ethiopian quality.

🤝

Community

We work directly with local producers to support Ethiopian livelihoods and preserve traditional craftsmanship for future generations.

🔒

Trust

Secure payments, transparent pricing, and reliable delivery across all regions of Ethiopia. Your satisfaction is our priority.
