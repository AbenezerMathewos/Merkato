❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about shopping at MERKATO.

🛒 Ordering 💳 Payment 🚚 Delivery 🔄 Returns 📦 Products 👤 Account

🛒 Ordering

How do I place an order?

Browse our Supermarket Floor, click on an item to view details, then click "Add to Cart" and proceed to checkout.

Can I modify my order after placing it?

You can modify your order within 30 minutes of placing it. Contact our support team at support@merkato.com.

How do I cancel an order?

Go to My Orders, find your order, and click "Cancel Order". Orders can only be cancelled before they're shipped.

Can I order in bulk or wholesale?

Yes! Contact our wholesale team at wholesale@merkato.com for bulk order inquiries and special pricing.

💳 Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Telebirr mobile money, Cash on Delivery (COD), Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard), and Bank Transfer.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes! We use encrypted payment processing and never store your full payment details on our servers.

Do you offer installment payments?

We're working on installment payment options. Currently, we offer "Pay in 30 days" for orders over 20,000 ETB.

What is Telebirr and how do I use it?

Telebirr is Ethiopia's leading mobile money service. You can pay directly from your phone using the Telebirr app. Simply select Telebirr at checkout and follow the prompts.

🚚 Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Addis Ababa: 1-3 days. Regional areas: 3-7 days. Remote areas may take up to 10 days.

How much does shipping cost?

Free shipping on orders over 3,000 ETB. Flat rate of 200 ETB for orders under 3,000 ETB.

Do you deliver internationally?

Not yet. We currently deliver within Ethiopia only. International shipping will be available in 2027.

Can I track my delivery?

Yes! Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via SMS and email. You can also track it in My Orders.

🔄 Returns

What is your return policy?

You can return items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be in original condition. See our Returns Policy for details.

How do I return an item?

Go to the Returns page, fill out the return form, and we'll provide you with return instructions.

How long does a refund take?

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.

📦 Products

Are your products authentic?

Yes! We source directly from Ethiopian artisans, farmers, and manufacturers. Every product is verified for authenticity.

Can I request a product not in your catalog?

Absolutely! Email us at suggestions@merkato.com and we'll try to source it for you.

Are your products organic and ethically sourced?

We prioritize working with producers who use sustainable and ethical practices. Many of our products are organic, and we ensure fair prices for all producers.

👤 Account

How do I create an account?

Go to the Sign In page and fill out the "Create Account" form. It only takes a minute!

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Click "Forgot Password" on the Sign In page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

How do I delete my account?

Contact our support team at support@merkato.com with your account details. We'll process your request within 3 business days.

Can I have multiple delivery addresses?

Yes! You can save multiple addresses in your account profile and choose the one you want at checkout.

💬 Still Need Help?

Didn't find the answer you're looking for? We're here to help!
